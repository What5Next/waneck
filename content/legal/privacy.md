# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 11, 2026**

This Policy explains how whatsnext handles information used to provide its AI character chat service. For privacy questions, contact whatsnext.public@gmail.com.

## 1. Information we collect

We process account and profile information; characters you create; messages and saved conversation history; Nex balance and purchase records; support requests; and technical information such as IP address, browser information, and service logs. Please do not enter sensitive personal information that is unnecessary for a character conversation.

## 2. How we use information

We use this information to provide accounts and AI chats, save your content and conversations, manage Nex purchases, answer support requests, protect the service from misuse, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

## 3. AI conversations and service providers

Your messages and relevant conversation context are sent to Google’s Gemini service to generate replies. We use Supabase for account and application data. The payment provider shown at checkout processes your payment. These providers may process information outside your country. We share information with them only as needed for their services.

We may review reported content and related records when investigating safety concerns, misuse, or a support request. Do not treat an AI conversation as confidential professional advice.

## 4. Retention and deletion

We keep account information and saved conversations while needed to provide the service. When you request account deletion, we will delete or anonymize information that is no longer needed. We may retain certain records where necessary for legal obligations, security, fraud prevention, or payment disputes.

To request access, correction, or deletion of your information, email whatsnext.public@gmail.com from the address associated with your account. We may need to verify that the account belongs to you.

## 5. Your rights and security

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights to access, correct, delete, export, or restrict the use of your personal information, or to complain to a privacy regulator. Contact us to make a request.

We use reasonable measures to protect information, although no online service is completely secure. We may update this Policy and will notify users of material changes where appropriate.
