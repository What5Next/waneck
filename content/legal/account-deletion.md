# Account & Data Deletion

You can ask us to delete your whatsnext account and the information associated with it at any time.

## How to request deletion

Email whatsnext.public@gmail.com from the address associated with your account and tell us you would like your account deleted. We may need to verify that the account belongs to you before we proceed.

## What happens next

When you request account deletion, we will delete or anonymize information that is no longer needed. We may retain certain records where necessary for legal obligations, security, fraud prevention, or payment disputes.

Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to access, correct, export, or restrict the use of your personal information. Contact us to make a request. See our [Privacy Policy](/privacy) for details.
