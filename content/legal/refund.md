# Refund Policy

**Last updated: October 11, 2026**

## 1. Nex purchases

Nex packages are one-time digital purchases. The amount of Nex, any bonus, and the total price are shown before payment. Free or promotional Nex has no cash value.

## 2. Requesting a refund

You may request a refund within **7 days of purchase** by emailing whatsnext.public@gmail.com. Include your account email, purchase date, transaction reference, and the reason for your request. Do not send your full payment card number.

## 3. Refund eligibility

Unused purchased Nex is eligible for a refund within the 7-day request period. If purchased Nex has been used, the purchase is generally not refundable. We will consider requests involving technical problems, a purchase that was not delivered as described, or other circumstances where a refund is required by applicable law.

This Policy does not limit any cancellation, withdrawal, or other consumer rights that cannot legally be waived in your location.

## 4. Processing

We will review your request and explain the outcome by email. Approved refunds are returned through the original payment channel where possible. The time for funds to appear depends on the payment provider. Nex associated with a refunded purchase may be removed from your balance.
